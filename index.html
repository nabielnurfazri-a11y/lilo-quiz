MY EXOTIC ANIMAL FRIEND, LILO.

My most special exotic pet is an axolotl named Lilo, a unique aquatic creature often called the "Mexican walking fish." Even though it has "fish" in its nickname, Lilo is actually an amphibian from the freshwater lakes near Mexico City. Unlike regular frogs that grow up and move onto land, Lilo spends its entire life underwater in my home aquarium, making it a truly cool and unusual friend to keep.

Looking at Lilo is like having a cute, miniature dragon living in my room. It has a flat head, tiny dark eyes, and a wide mouth that always looks like a sweet, permanent smile. Six fluffy pink gills branch out from its head to help it breathe, while its smooth body and four small legs help it walk along the tank floor. Lilo is a very calm pet that loves floating gracefully, resting on smooth rocks, and quickly vacuuming up worms at mealtime. The coolest part is that it can even regrow its own legs and tail if they get hurt, which makes my smiling little friend feel totally magical!
